Tone.SequenceSwitch hot-swaps the instrument a Sequence plays into during playback. The old instrument only finishes notes that have already started their release; all subsequent events go to the new instrument. This demo plays 16 eighth notes: the first 8 through a triangle-wave Synth, the last 8 through a square-wave Synth. The switch happens on beat 2 so the triangle's tail is audible while the square takes over.
